# Product Decisions Register

## 1. Purpose

This register records the founder-approved product decisions that define the initial direction of ZAR HORIZON AI @WORK.

All decisions below are marked as FOUNDER APPROVED.

## 2. Decision register

### Decision 1: Two separate AI products

Status: FOUNDER APPROVED

The company will build two independent AI-powered digital employees:

- AI Business Partner
- AI Hacker

These are separate products with distinct domains, operational boundaries, and shared platform support.

### Decision 2: Both agents must execute actual work

Status: FOUNDER APPROVED

The products must move beyond recommendation-only behaviour. They must be able to research, reason, use tools, perform approved work, verify outcomes, and report results.

### Decision 3: Shared platform with independent agent engines

Status: FOUNDER APPROVED

Both products will use a shared platform for common enterprise capabilities, but each agent must remain independently governed and logically distinct.

### Decision 4: South Africa as the initial target market

Status: FOUNDER APPROVED

The initial commercial and customer focus is South Africa, with the intention to expand across Africa later.

### Decision 5: Evidence-backed results

Status: FOUNDER APPROVED

Business and security results must be traceable, evidence-backed, and explainable. Findings and recommendations must distinguish facts, estimates, assumptions, and validated outcomes.

### Decision 6: Human approval for sensitive operations

Status: FOUNDER APPROVED

Sensitive operations, especially external communications, financial actions, and high-risk security operations, require explicit approval and oversight as part of the operating model.

### Decision 7: Advanced offensive security capabilities within authorised scope

Status: FOUNDER APPROVED

The AI Hacker is intended to perform advanced, authorised security investigation and validation, including offensive security testing within a controlled and approved scope.

### Decision 8: Existing AI models initially rather than training our own foundation model

Status: FOUNDER APPROVED

The company will initially use existing AI models rather than training a new foundational model in the early phase.

## 3. Non-approved or future decisions

The following items are not recorded as founder-approved decisions unless explicitly approved later:

- specific vendor integrations,
- exact business software providers,
- specific external AI model choices beyond the general use of existing models,
- detailed tool stack for security testing,
- specific commercial workflows beyond the approved domain statements,
- detailed rollout strategy for Africa-wide expansion.

These remain open product decisions for future review.

## 4. Decision discipline

This register is intentionally limited to founder-approved decisions. It does not claim approval for future product features that have not yet been formally endorsed.

The founder-approved vision takes priority over generic assumptions or market trends.
