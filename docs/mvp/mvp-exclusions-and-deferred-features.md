# MVP exclusions and deferred features

## Objective

Document what is intentionally excluded from the first release of each product and what should be deferred pending technical, commercial or customer validation.

## AI Business Partner exclusions and deferrals

### Excluded from the initial MVP

- fully autonomous management of all external business communications;
- broad autonomous supplier negotiation and purchasing commitments;
- deep legal or tax advice as a product promise;
- complete agency-level financial advisory or regulated financial services;
- full ERP-scale implementation and multi-entity financial control;
- broad autonomous workflow orchestration across every business stack without clear approval.

### Deferred because of complexity

- full multi-source market research and competitor intelligence at scale;
- full supplier discovery across complex procurement scenarios;
- continuous operational monitoring across every business system;
- advanced multi-entity forecasting and scenario planning;
- full automated CRM and prospect orchestration across lots of external tools.

### Deferred because of integration dependency

- third-party accounting integrations beyond a limited, controlled set;
- CRM and contact syncing at enterprise scale;
- complex procurement and supplier integrations;
- external messaging integrations requiring complex governance.

### Requires customer validation

- whether customers prefer a sales-focused workflow over a business performance workflow;
- whether they want a broader “all-in-one business partner” or a narrower operational assistant;
- whether customers prioritise finance, sales or tasks as the first use case;
- whether they expect autonomous action or managed approval-driven action.

### Requires specialist human involvement

- regulated financial review;
- legal sign-off for contracts and external commitments;
- tax planning or formal compliance review;
- complex supplier negotiations with higher-risk commercial exposure.

## AI Hacker exclusions and deferrals

### Excluded from the initial MVP

- destructive exploitation;
- denial-of-service testing;
- unauthorised or out-of-scope network scans;
- credential theft or credential misuse;
- persistence or unauthorised backdoor creation;
- broad lateral movement beyond approved scope;
- testing that could affect availability or sensitive data without explicit authorisation.

### Deferred because of complexity

- deep cloud attack-path analysis across many environments;
- broad network infrastructure and segmentation review at enterprise scale;
- continuous monitoring at a high-autonomy level;
- highly advanced privilege escalation research in complex estates;
- large-scale multi-tenant security program management.

### Deferred because of integration dependency

- full cloud integration and cloud security coverage without explicit access and governance;
- complex identity-provider and infrastructure integration requirements;
- deep ticketing and workflow integration beyond a controlled initial implementation.

### Requires customer validation

- how much autonomous testing customers want versus human-assisted validation;
- whether the first commercial target is SaaS, MSPs, software teams, or broader security customers;
- whether customers are more interested in web app/API assessment or broader infrastructure review first;
- the acceptable balance between self-service and project-led delivery.

### Requires specialist human involvement

- risk acceptance for high-severity or high-impact findings;
- legal or contractual review where testing touches regulated systems;
- high-risk exploitation or escalation tests;
- customer-specific security board reviews for major findings.

## Strategic note

These exclusions are not a retreat from long-term product vision. They are a discipline to ensure the first release is useful, safe, trustworthy and commercially defensible.
