# AI Business Partner feature prioritisation

## Priority scale

- P0 = Essential foundation
- P1 = MVP differentiator
- P2 = Post-MVP
- P3 = Future research
- Excluded = outside agreed product scope or not justified in the first release

## Feature matrix

| Feature | Priority | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Business profile and context setup | P0 | Required to orient the agent and structure all following business analysis. |
| Business information ingestion | P0 | The product needs a clean business data foundation to support analysis and execution. |
| Revenue and expense analysis | P0 | Highly frequent and valuable; directly supports SME financial visibility. |
| Cash-flow visibility and summary | P0 | Critical business pain point and core operational value. |
| Profitability and margin analysis | P0 | Strong customer value and directly tied to business decision quality. |
| Business performance reporting | P0 | A core output for business owners and operators. |
| Business document generation | P0 | Practical, repeated and useful across many workflows. |
| Task creation and coordination | P0 | Essential for the “execution” element of the AI Business Partner. |
| Approval-gated external actions | P0 | Required for trust, safety and operational control. |
| Execution verification | P0 | Required to prove that actions happened and whether they produced results. |
| Market and competitor research | P1 | Valuable but can be scoped to selected business contexts rather than universal use. |
| Supplier discovery and comparison | P1 | Useful in operations and procurement but not required for the initial core workflow. |
| Sales lead organisation and follow-up | P1 | Valuable for sales-led businesses but not universal across all SMEs. |
| Quotation preparation | P1 | Clear value but more dependent on pricing logic and user approval. |
| Budget vs actual analysis | P1 | Useful for finance-led and operational firms, but can be a second-wave feature. |
| Improvement recommendations | P1 | Good for differentiation but depends on data quality and business context. |
| Advanced forecasting | P2 | Valuable but requires stronger assumptions and data quality. |
| Full multi-entity financial consolidation | P2 | Useful for larger corporates, but not the core SME MVP. |
| Deep partner and supplier intelligence | P2 | Higher complexity and more research-intensive. |
| Broad autonomous outreach across multiple channels | P2 | Useful only with stronger governance, permissions and trust controls. |
| Full multi-channel customer intelligence | P3 | Requires strong data and research tooling; more speculative for early launch. |
| Full enterprise ERP integration matrix | P3 | High dependency and implementation burden. |
| Fully autonomous regulatory compliance advice | Excluded | Too risky and outside the protected product scope. |
| Broad legal or tax advisory capability | Excluded | Not appropriate for the product definition and would require specialist human review. |

## Why P0 and P1 matter most

P0 features define the workable first product. They are the capabilities required to create a useful business operating workflow without turning the product into a generic advisor.

P1 features add differentiation and user value, but they can be gated behind a narrower product scope and controlled rollout.

## Recommended classification logic

The primary MVP should be built around the P0 set, with P1 features added selectively based on the chosen initial customer segment and workflow.
