# Day 7 MVP decisions

## Summary

This document captures the working MVP recommendations for the two specialist products in ZAR HORIZON AI @WORK.

It does not convert recommendations into approved product direction. It records the proposed first-release boundaries, the rationale, and the decisions that still require founder approval.

## Recommended AI Business Partner MVP

Primary workflow:

- business performance and action agent

Core value proposition:

- help a business understand performance, identify issues or opportunities, and execute an approved action or document with verification.

Essential MVP components:

- business profile and context;
- business information ingestion;
- financial analysis and performance reporting;
- task and action creation with approval gating;
- execution verification;
- document generation and workflow support.

## Recommended AI Hacker MVP

Primary workflow:

- authorised web application and API security assessment

Core value proposition:

- perform an authorised security review of the web app and API surface, validate findings, guide remediation, and verify that the fix worked.

Essential MVP components:

- organisation and asset registration;
- authorisation and scope control;
- scoped discovery and assessment;
- vulnerability validation and evidence capture;
- remediation guidance;
- retesting and verification;
- structured reporting.

## Shared platform foundations

The first release should include only the shared platform foundations needed to support the two specialist products safely and credibly:

- identity and authentication;
- organisation and workspace management;
- tenant separation;
- role-based permissions;
- subscription and entitlement framework;
- audit logging;
- execution history;
- approval engine;
- document storage;
- notifications;
- AI provider abstraction;
- tool policy controls.

## Main future exclusions and deferrals

- deep multi-system orchestration beyond the initial workflow;
- broad continuous monitoring without clear customer contract;
- high-risk security testing beyond the approved scope;
- broad uncontrolled business automation or external actioning;
- full enterprise ERP and complex multi-entity breadth.

## Strategic recommendation

The MVP should be narrow, complete and valuable. It should demonstrate that the product can operate end-to-end, produce trusted outputs, and stay within controlled boundaries.

This approach best preserves the founder-approved vision without prematurely assuming the product is ready for a broad or fully autonomous operating model.

## Approval status

These are all recommendations for founder review. They are not founder-approved implementation decisions.
