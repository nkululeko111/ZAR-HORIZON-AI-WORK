# MVP success metrics

## Purpose

This document defines the measurable outcomes that would help validate whether the MVPs for the AI Business Partner and AI Hacker are delivering useful value in a real-world setting.

The metrics below are framed as proposed measures for pilot evaluation. They are not claims of current performance.

## AI Business Partner MVP success metrics

### Core operational metrics

- time saved on recurring business tasks;
- percentage of generated documents or reports accepted without major revisions;
- successful completion rate of approved workflow tasks;
- proportion of analyses that used verified rather than assumed data;
- execution verification rate for approved actions;
- user repeat-usage rate over time;
- customer willingness to pay or extend usage after pilot tests.

### More detailed signals

- number of finance or operations issues identified by the agent that the user then acted on;
- reduction in manual admin time for quote preparation, follow-up coordination, and reporting;
- quality of financial or operational recommendations as judged by user review;
- percentage of generated tasks that had an actual outcome or follow-up action recorded;
- user satisfaction with action explanation quality and evidence quality.

### Pilot-only validation

Some metrics can only be fully validated after live pilots, including:

- repeat usage and customer retention;
- revenue impact or cash-flow improvements attributable to agent actions;
- time-to-value in real SME environments;
- trust in generated business advice and documents.

## AI Hacker MVP success metrics

### Core security metrics

- validated finding rate;
- false-positive rate;
- evidence completeness rate;
- assessment completion rate;
- time to identify and validate relevant findings;
- remediation verification rate;
- safety and scope compliance rate;
- customer willingness to pay after pilot use.

### More detailed signals

- percentage of findings that are confirmed rather than suspected;
- proportion of reported vulnerabilities supported by reproduction steps and evidence;
- number of findings closed by retesting after remediation;
- number of safety incidents or authorisation breaches avoided;
- average time from assessment start to first actionable output;
- time taken to reach a verified remediation decision.

### Pilot-only validation

The following metrics require real pilot conditions and should not be treated as current or achieved targets:

- sustained reduction in exploitable risk;
- customer trust in severity labels and evidence quality;
- remediation effectiveness over time;
- customer retention and expansion for security services;
- reduction in time spent by internal security teams on repeated manual validation.

## Shared metric principles

Across both products, success should be measured by:

- whether the workflow solves a real problem,
- whether the output is evidence-backed,
- whether the action or finding was verified,
- whether the customer wants to continue using it,
- whether the product stayed within scope and approval boundaries.

## Important caveat

No success metrics in this document should be treated as achieved. They are proposed pilot metrics that must be validated in real customer and operational conditions.
