# AI Business Partner pricing benchmark

## Objective

This benchmark reviews public pricing and commercial patterns in the accounting, CRM and business software market. It is intended to understand how competitors structure pricing and where South African SMEs may expect to pay.

## Public pricing observations

- Other Sage products, including larger enterprise forms, are presented as price on request or via solution-specific quotation.
- Source status: Verified vendor-reported pricing for the entry product; enterprise pricing is not public in the accessible page.

## Pricing patterns observed

### 1. SMB SaaS is dominant

SMB products generally rely on:

- monthly or annual subscriptions;
- per-user or per-organisation pricing;
- add-ons such as users, locations or automation features;
- free trials or introductory offers.

### 2. Local African pricing is available but uneven

Products such as Zoho Books and Sage clearly publish local pricing, while some premium or enterprise platforms are more quote-driven.

### 3. More advanced products cost more than traditional accounting tools

Sales and ERP platforms push into higher tiers quickly as user count, automation or integrations increase.

### 4. AI is often a feature layer, not a separate product category

AI is often bundled into a broader subscription rather than sold as a separate autonomous-execution layer.

## Commercial interpretation

The market shows credible SaaS pricing for SMB operations. The likely commercial environment for a product like ZAR HORIZON AI @WORK would be a combination of:

- subscription pricing for smaller businesses;
- usage or user-based pricing for larger organisations;
- project-based or managed-service pricing for more advanced consulting-style assessments or custom implementations;
- enterprise add-ons for integrations, compliance and security-sensitive workflows.

## Key takeaway

The strongest competitors are not “cheap”; they are often modular and highly segmented. A differentiated market opportunity exists for a purpose-built business operating partner that connects business intelligence with action-taking and verification, especially where smaller businesses need value without enterprise implementation overhead.
