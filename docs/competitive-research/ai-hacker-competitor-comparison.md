## Interpretation

The most credible competitors are not the ones simply “using AI.” The strongest benchmarks are the products that combine:

- explicit scope control;
- evidence-backed findings;
- exploit or validation workflows;
- remediation support;
- retesting or validation after remediation;
- clear separation between confirmed, suspected, and unvalidated issues.

This is the most important difference between a generic scanner and a genuine AI-enabled testing platform.
