## Summary interpretation

The direct competitive pattern is clear: the market has mature software for finance, CRM and operations, but few solutions combine business intelligence, evidence-backed actions, and multi-domain execution in a single localised business-facing AI assistant.

This indicates a meaningful opportunity for ZAR HORIZON AI @WORK to focus on the “research-to-execution” layer rather than simply competing on accounting or CRM feature count.
