# Business Partner Customer Scenarios

## 1. Purpose

This document provides illustrative product scenarios for the AI Business Partner. These are not customer interview records or validated commercial findings. They are examples of how the product could support different South African business contexts in a way that is consistent with the approved vision.

## 2. Scenario 1: Township retail business

### Customer situation

A township retail shop sells groceries and household goods with a small team and limited accounting support.

### Business problem

The owner struggles to know which products sell well, how much cash is available, and which suppliers offer the best value. Inventory is tracked manually and sales are inconsistent.

### What the AI Business Partner investigates

- sales trends across product categories,
- stock movement patterns,
- supplier cost comparisons,
- revenue and expense fluctuations,
- likely fast-moving products and slow sellers.

### What decisions it helps the owner make

- which products to reorder,
- where pricing may be too low or too high,
- whether stock is tied up in slow-moving items,
- which supplier options are more cost-effective.

### What tasks it actually performs

- prepares a weekly sales and stock summary,
- compares supplier quotes,
- creates a reorder recommendation,
- drafts a simple business performance report,
- schedules follow-up tasks and reminders.

### Approvals needed

- approval before sending supplier quotations or committing to supplier changes,
- approval before sending customer-facing messaging or financial decisions.

### How the result is measured

- reduced stockouts,
- improved sales visibility,
- lower cost of stockholding,
- faster reorder decisions.

### What the agent investigates

- sales pipeline and follow-up status,
- project profitability,
- supplier cost variability,
- customer retention and repeat engagement.

### What decisions it helps the owner make

- which clients are worth pursuing,
- which offerings are most profitable,
- which follow-ups should happen this week,
- whether pricing needs revision.

### What tasks it actually performs

- prepares quotation drafts,
- summarises recent sales and margin performance,
- tracks follow-up tasks,
- drafts customer communications,
- identifies which customers require attention.

### Approvals needed

- approval before sending price quotes or customer-facing commitments,
- approval to update customer or supplier records.

### How the result is measured

- faster quote turnaround,
- more consistent follow-up,
- better visibility into margins and customer repeat business.

## 10. Illustrative product value

Across these scenarios, the AI Business Partner is valuable when it helps the owner move from:

- data and fragmented processes,
- to structured business understanding,
- to execution and follow-up,
- to clear performance feedback and decision support.

The common pattern is not “AI advice” alone. The value is in closing the gap between insight and action.
