# Customer Outreach Templates

## 1. Purpose

These templates are for founder-led customer discovery. The objective is to learn about customer pain, not to pitch an unfinished product or pressure a prospect into a meeting.

Use a professional, conversational tone. Keep messages short, clear, and respectful of time.

## 2. WhatsApp introduction

Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] from ZAR HORIZON AI @WORK. I am doing some customer discovery in South Africa to understand how businesses and technical teams are currently dealing with operational efficiency and security challenges.

I would value 15–20 minutes to learn more about your experience, especially around [problem area]. No pitch—just a short conversation to understand the real pain points and whether we are solving the right problems.

Would you be open to a short chat sometime this week or next?

## 3. LinkedIn connection message

Hi [Name], I came across your profile and was interested in your work in [industry/role]. I am exploring how businesses and technical teams in South Africa are handling [specific challenge], and I would value understanding your experience.

I am not pitching a product yet—just trying to learn from real operators and decision-makers about the problems they face and where the pain is most acute.

Would you be open to a brief conversation sometime in the next week?

## 4. LinkedIn follow-up

Hi [Name], thanks for connecting. I appreciate your time. I am doing customer discovery around [specific challenge] and would really value a brief conversation with someone who understands the practical realities of this area.

I am hoping to learn:

- how the problem shows up in real operations,
- what current solutions are being used,
- where the pain is greatest,
- what the cost or consequences are when it is not solved.

If you are open to it, I would appreciate 15–20 minutes. If now is not the right time, I would still welcome any practical insight you can share.

## 5. Email interview request

Subject: Brief customer interview request

Hi [Name],

I hope you are well.

I am reaching out as part of a customer discovery exercise for ZAR HORIZON AI @WORK. We are trying to understand how businesses and technical teams in South Africa are dealing with [problem area], and I would value learning from your experience.

This is not a sales conversation. It is a short discussion focused on the real challenges, current tools, and operational pressures you face.

I would be grateful for 15–20 minutes at a time that suits you. If that is not possible, I would still appreciate any practical insight you can share.

Thank you for your time and perspective.

Best,
[Your Name]
[Role / Company]
[Contact details]

## 6. Follow-up after no response

Hi [Name], just checking in on my previous message. I understand you may be busy, and I do not want to take too much of your time.

I am only looking for a short conversation to better understand how [specific challenge] is currently handled in your environment.

If there is no fit, I completely understand. If you know someone who would be a useful perspective, I would be grateful for a referral.

Thank you for your time.

## 7. Thank-you message after an interview

Hi [Name],

Thank you for taking the time to speak with me. Your perspective was very valuable and helped clarify how this challenge is actually experienced in practice.

I appreciate the honesty about the current tools, constraints, and trade-offs involved. It will help us better understand which problems are most important to solve and which approaches are more likely to be useful.

I would be glad to stay in touch if we continue exploring this area, and I appreciate your time and insight.

Best,
[Your Name]
